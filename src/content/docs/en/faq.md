## Getting Started

### How can I join the server?
You need FiveM and GTA V. Search for "Capitalyx" in the FiveM server list.

### Do I need to apply to play?
No, the server is open access. You can connect directly and create your character.

### How many characters can I create?
You can create one character per FiveM account.

## Gameplay

### What is a tycoon server?
A server focused on business management, jobs and the economy. You can grow your business empire in a relaxed atmosphere.

### Can I play with my friends?
Absolutely! Mutual help and cooperation are encouraged. You can start businesses together or trade resources.

### How do I earn money?
Choose a job you like, grow your business, trade with other players.

## Technical

### The server is lagging, what should I do?
Check your internet connection. If the problem persists, report it on Discord with precise details.

### I lost my items/money, what should I do?
Contact an administrator on Discord with evidence (screenshots, videos) and details of the incident.

### How do I report a bug?
Use the #bug-reports channel on Discord with a detailed description and evidence if possible.

## Rules and Sanctions

### What happens if I break a rule?
Depending on severity: warning, temporary kick or permanent ban. See the [Rules](/en/rules/) page for details.

### How do I appeal a sanction?
Open a ticket on Discord in the appropriate section with your arguments and evidence.

### Can I appeal a ban?
Yes, after a cooldown period. Use the Discord ticket system to submit your appeal request.

## Community

### How do I join the Discord?
The Discord link is available in-game via the `/discord` command or on our website.

### Are there organized events?
Yes! Economic events, production contests, special markets and community challenges are regularly organized.

### How do I create an alliance/business?
You can create or join a faction/business to benefit from XP bonuses and other light perks that ease your progression.

### What are the benefits of factions?
Factions mainly offer experience bonuses, light economic advantages and a mutual-help environment between members.

## Jobs & Economy

### What jobs are available?
See the [Jobs](/en/jobs/) section of the wiki for the full list and details on each one.

### Can players trade resources?
Yes! Trading and mutual help are at the heart of the gameplay. You can sell your production or help other players with your resources.

### How does the economic system work?
The server's economy is fixed with established prices and mechanics. Focus on optimizing your production and job efficiency.

## Need more help?

If you can't find the answer to your question here, feel free to:

- Ask your question on Discord
- Contact a staff member in-game
- Open a support ticket
