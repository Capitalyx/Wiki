# Beginner Guide - Capitalyx

Welcome to your adventure on Capitalyx! This guide will help you take your first steps on the server.

## 🚀 First Steps

### 1. Connecting to the Server
- Download FiveM from [fivem.net](https://fivem.net)
- Search for "Capitalyx" in the FiveM server list
- Join the server and create your character

### 2. Character Creation
- Choose your character's appearance
- Select a realistic name
- Set your character's age and origin

## 💼 Choosing a Job

### Legal Jobs
- **Bus Driver**: Transport citizens around the city
- **Courier**: Handle deliveries for various businesses
- **Pilot**: Transport passengers by air
- **Train Conductor**: Ensure rail transport

### Illegal Jobs
- **Trafficker**: Trade in illicit substances (risky)
- **Thief**: Burglaries and theft (high police attention)

## 🏠 Housing

- Visit the real estate agency to buy a property
- Prices vary depending on location
- You can customize your interior

## 🚗 Transport

- **Cars**: Buy from a dealership
- **Public transport**: Free buses and trains
- **Taxis**: Fast transport service

## 💰 Economy

### Earning Money
1. Work regularly at your job
2. Invest in real estate
3. Start your own business
4. Take part in server events

### Spending Money
- Food and drinks
- Clothes and accessories
- Vehicles and upgrades
- Real estate

## 🎭 Roleplay Tips

### Do's ✅
- Always stay in character
- Interact with other players
- Follow the server rules
- Create interesting stories

### Don'ts ❌
- Don't break roleplay
- Don't use out-of-game information
- Don't troll other players
- Don't neglect the rules
