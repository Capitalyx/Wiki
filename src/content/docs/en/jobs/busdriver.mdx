## Where to find this job

Head to the **bus station** to get started.

![Bus station location](/assets/img/jobs/busdriver/Zone.png)
![Bus station map](/assets/img/jobs/busdriver/Map.png)

## How to start

1. **Take the bus out of the garage** — Access your garage and take out the bus to start your day.
2. **Go to the bus station** — Head to the bus station and select the route of your choice.
3. **Complete the route** — Finish the trip while respecting the stops to receive your payment.
