## Where to find this job

Head to the **taxi center** to get your license and rent your first vehicle. It's located just south of the Casino, east of the city.

![Taxi building](/assets/img/jobs/cab/cabbuilding.webp)
![Taxi center location](/assets/img/jobs/cab/cabmap.png)

## How to start

1. **Go to the dealership** — Pick up your first vehicle (free).
2. **Choose your route** — Pick a route based on your level.
3. **Pick up customers** — Follow the marker on the map to pick up and drive the customer.
4. **Multiple customers** — You can have several stops before finishing your shift.
5. **Receive your reward** — Collect your end-of-shift bonus payment.
6. **Repeat the process** — Chain rides to maximize your earnings.
