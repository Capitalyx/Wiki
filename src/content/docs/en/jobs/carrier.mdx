## Where to find this job

The carrier job is available from two depots: **Paleto Bay** and the **Terminal**, in the Los Santos port area. Head to either one to get your badge and pick up your first delivery vehicle.

## How to start

1. **Pick up the vehicle** — Activate the garage marker to spawn your delivery vehicle.
2. **Pick up the packages** — Follow the marker to load the packages into your vehicle.
3. **Deliver the packages** — Deliver the packages to the indicated addresses and collect your payment.
