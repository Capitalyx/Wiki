## How to start

1. **Get an EMS vehicle** — Have an EMS vehicle in your garage or buy one from the dealership.
2. **Head to a hospital** — Drive your EMS vehicle to any hospital in the city.
3. **Start your shift** — Start your shift and wait a few seconds for a patient to be assigned to you.
4. **Pick up the patient** — Head to the indicated location to pick up and transport the patient.

## Streak and earnings

The bigger your streak, the more money you earn. If you end a shift at a hospital, you can go to another hospital and the streak will continue. However, it breaks if you change jobs or disconnect.
