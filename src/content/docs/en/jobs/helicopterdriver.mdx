## How to start

0. **Preamble** — Have a helicopter in your aircraft garage, otherwise buy one from the aircraft dealership.
1. **Take out a helicopter** — Take a helicopter out of the aircraft garage.
2. **Head to the marina** — Go to the marina at the yellow point marked "NORTH".
3. **Start the mission** — Start the mission by standing at the arrival point.
