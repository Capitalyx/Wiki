## Where to find this job

Head to a mechanic garage to start repairing and towing players' vehicles.

## How to start

1. **Start your shift** — Head to the garage to start your mechanic shift.
2. **Answer breakdown calls** — Follow the marker to reach the broken-down vehicle.
3. **Repair or tow** — Repair the vehicle on-site or tow it to the garage depending on the situation.
