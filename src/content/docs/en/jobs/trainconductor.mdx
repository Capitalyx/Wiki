## Where to find this job

Head to the **central station**, in the center of the city, to get your conductor certification and access the locomotives.

## How to start

1. **Access the locomotive** — Activate the depot marker to spawn your locomotive.
2. **Load passengers** — Follow the route and stop at stations to board passengers.
3. **Complete the trip** — Respect the schedule and stops to receive your payment.
