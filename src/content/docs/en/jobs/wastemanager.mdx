## Where to find this job

Head to the **South L.S. Recycling & Document Destruction** center, in south Los Santos, to start your waste collection shift.

## How to start

1. **Start your shift** — Head to the recycling center to start your collection shift.
2. **Collect waste** — Follow the marker to pick up waste at the indicated points.
3. **Drop off the waste** — Bring the collected waste back to the center to receive your payment.
